1. Executive Summary
ReliefHub
 AI is a closed-loop local resource management platform designed for a single NGO Hub. It shifts humanitarian aid from reactive distribution to proactive resource balancing by combining real-time inventory management, AI-driven scarcity prediction, a two-mode response system, and a self-service donor platform for future needs.
1.1 The Problem
NGOs operating at the neighborhood level consistently face two simultaneous failures:
Scarcity Blindness: 
They learn about shortages only after beneficiaries arrive empty-handed, with no forward-looking signal.
Abundance Waste: 
Donors in resource-rich neighborhoods give randomly — often duplicating items already in excess while critical goods run out.
Reactive Operations: 
Staff spend time reacting to crises rather than anticipating them, leading to burnout and inefficiency.
1.2 The Solution
ReliefHub
 AI Core Concept
A geo-sectored, AI-powered resource loop with two distinct response modes:  Mode 1 — Immediate Response: When Scout identifies CRITICAL or 
HIGH-risk
 sectors, the NGO distributes directly from its own Storehouse inventory. No donation drive is 
created;
 the Ledger handles fulfilment and updates runway in real time.  Mode 2 — Proactive Replenishment: When Scout identifies MONITOR or WARNING runway trajectories (predicted shortfall, not yet critical), The Replenisher creates a Donation Drive visible in the Donor app. Donors browse and pledge at their own pace for upcoming needs. No urgency push is sent to donors.

3. Feature Breakdown
3.1 The Scout — AI Trend Analysis Engine
Mission
Detect early-warning signals of resource scarcity across the NGO's 25 sectors before any beneficiary reports a shortage. The Scout uses internal 
Firestore
 data (consumption trends, runway acceleration, check-in spikes) as the primary 
signal source —
 processed by Gemini 1.5 Pro. External signals (weather events, local news) serve only as secondary triggers that initiate an unscheduled Scout re-scan, never as direct inputs to the risk score.
3.1.1 Signal Sources — Primary (Internal)
All primary signals are sourced directly from 
Firestore
 and are ground-truth, sector-specific, and already structured — making Gemini's risk assessment anchored to verifiable operational data rather than uncertain external coverage.
Consumption Acceleration: 
14-day rolling 
avg_daily_consumption
 per SKU compared against the prior 14-day period. A >25% acceleration in any category flags a trend anomaly for that sector
Runway Trajectory: 
Rate of runway decline per SKU — a SKU dropping from 20 days to 12 days runway in 7 days is a stronger signal than one sitting at 8 days stable
Beneficiary Check-in Spike: 
Unusual increase in beneficiary registrations or distribution requests logged to a sector within a rolling 7-day window
Stock-out Event Frequency: 
Number 
of times a
 SKU hit zero 
stock
 in the last 30 days for a given sector — a pattern indicator, not just a current-state snapshot
Distribution Request Gaps: 
Categories not requested at all in a sector for an unusually long period — may indicate beneficiaries have given up seeking aid (silent scarcity)
3.1.2 Signal Sources — Secondary (External Event Triggers Only)
External signals do NOT directly influence the risk score Gemini produces. Their sole role is to trigger an unscheduled Scout re-
scan
 outside the 6-hour 
cron
 window when a relevant event is detected. Gemini then reasons from internal data as normal.
Weather API flag: 
Extreme weather event (flood, drought, heatwave) detected in the NGO's district — triggers immediate Scout re-scan for all affected sectors
Google Custom Search: 
Keyword alert for '[City] food shortage', '[District] flood', '[Area] market 
closure' —
 if matched, triggers a re-scan for the relevant geographic cluster of sectors only
Earth Engine NDVI delta: 
Significant vegetation index 
drop
 in agricultural zones adjacent to the NGO radius — optional enrichment, triggers re-scan if delta exceeds threshold
Design Rationale
A news headline about 'Pune food shortage' cannot tell you whether Sector B3 or Sector D1 is affected — it is geographically imprecise and may lag reality by days. Internal consumption data, by contrast, is sector-specific and real-time. This architecture ensures Gemini's conclusions are always defensible with traceable 
Firestore
 evidence, not press coverage.

3.4 The Replenisher — Proactive Donation Drive Engine
Mission
The Replenisher operates exclusively on predicted future shortfalls — never on active crises. When The Scout identifies a MONITOR or WARNING runway trajectory (7–30 days remaining), The Replenisher creates a Donation Drive that appears in the donor app as a 
browseable
 feed. Donors contribute at their own pace, on their own schedule. There is no urgency push; the design philosophy is pre-emptive pipeline filling, not crisis crowdsourcing.
Two-Mode Response — Design Rationale
Mode 1 (CRITICAL / HIGH): NGO draws from its own Storehouse immediately. Initiating a donation drive 
at the moment
 of crisis is impractical — collection, transport, and sorting take days the NGO does not have. The Ledger handles this entirely.  Mode 2 (MONITOR / WARNING): With 7–30 days of runway remaining, there is enough time for a donation drive to be created, promoted, fulfilled, and restocked before the situation becomes critical. This is the only window where donor contribution is operationally meaningful.

FoodCloud
 (which connects surplus to charities reactively) or standard inventory tools (which only track what has already been distributed), 
ReliefHub
 AI separates immediate response (Storehouse → Ledger) from proactive replenishment (Scout → Replenisher → Donor Feed). Crises are handled with what the NGO already has. Donation drives are reserved for windows where they can 
actually work
.

Gemini 1.5 Pro is not just used for sentiment analysis — 
it
 reasons about inventory runway, geographic proximity, donation logistics, and campaign messaging in a single multi-modal prompt. This demonstrates genuine Build-with-AI integration.

Firestore

ReliefHub
 AI with a free-tier Firebase project.
